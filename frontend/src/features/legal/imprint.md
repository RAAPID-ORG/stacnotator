## Privacy

[OPERATOR_NAME] is the controller for personal data processed on STACNotator
(the "Service"), reachable at [CONTACT_EMAIL].

**What we store.** Your email address, display name, an identifier from the
authentication provider, and account timestamps. The annotations, campaigns,
and settings you create. API keys you add for imagery providers, encrypted at
rest and used only to fetch imagery on your behalf. Server-side IP addresses
and request metadata, kept short-term for operations and abuse detection. One
functional cookie, `tiler_token` (HttpOnly, one hour), which authorizes your
browser to load map tiles. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies.

**Why.** To provide the Service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to run it
securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Who else sees it.** Google Firebase Authentication handles sign-in
(https://policies.google.com/privacy); data may reach the United States under
the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Microsoft Azure hosts the Service, with data
stored in the West Europe region. Web fonts load from Google's font CDN, so
your IP address reaches Google when a page loads. For some imagery your browser
requests tiles directly from the provider, which likewise sees your IP address.
Administrators and, depending on settings, other members of your organizations
and campaigns see your email address and display name. We pass personal data to
no one else unless the law requires it.

**Retention and your rights.** We keep account and content data for as long as
your account exists. Write to [CONTACT_EMAIL] to have your account and its
personal data deleted, or to exercise your rights of access, rectification,
erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Annotations that campaigns
rely on may be kept in de-identified form. You may also complain to a data
protection supervisory authority.

We may update this notice when the Service's data flows change; the current
