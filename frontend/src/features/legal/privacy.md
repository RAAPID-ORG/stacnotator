**What we store.** Your email address, display name, an identifier from the
authentication provider, and account timestamps. The annotations, campaigns,
and settings you create. API keys you add for imagery providers, encrypted at
rest and used only to fetch imagery on your behalf. Server-side IP addresses
and request metadata, kept short-term for operations and abuse detection. One

browser to load map tiles. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies.
We do not sell personal information.

**Why.** To provide the Service you asked for and to run it securely. For users
in the EU, UK, and other regions with equivalent law, those purposes rest on
Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

**Who else sees it.** Google Firebase Authentication handles sign-in
(https://policies.google.com/privacy). Microsoft Azure hosts the Service, in
the [HOSTING_REGION] region; data of users outside that region is transferred
there, and transfers out of the EU rest on standard contractual clauses. Web
fonts load from Google's font CDN, so your IP address reaches Google when a
page loads. For some imagery your browser requests tiles directly from the
provider, which likewise sees your IP address. Administrators and, depending on
settings, other members of your organizations and campaigns see your email
address and display name. We pass personal data to no one else unless the law
requires it.

**Retention and your rights.** We keep account and content data for as long as
your account exists, and we may delete inactive accounts and projects (see
section 6 of the Terms of Service). Write to [CONTACT_EMAIL] to have your
account and its personal data deleted, or to request access, correction,
deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to processing. Annotations
that campaigns rely on may be kept in de-identified form. Users in the EU and
UK may also complain to their data protection supervisory authority.

**Children.** The Service is not directed at children under 16 and we do not
knowingly collect their data.
