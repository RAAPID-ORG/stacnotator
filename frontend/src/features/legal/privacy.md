## 2. What we process

**Account data.** When you sign in for the first time, we store your email
address, a display name, an identifier from the authentication provider, and
timestamps of account creation and last login. Your password is never stored by
the Service; authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication (see
section 4).

**Content data.** Annotations, campaigns, and related settings you create.
Organization and campaign administrators may add members by email address; if
someone adds your email, it is stored as part of that member list.

**Provider API keys.** Keys you add for third-party imagery providers are stored
encrypted at rest and used only to request imagery from that provider on your
behalf. They are visible to administrators of the organization or campaign they
belong to. See section 5 of the Terms of Service for who they belong to.

**Technical data.** Our servers process IP addresses and request metadata to
deliver the Service and to detect abuse. Server logs are kept short-term for
operations and security. The Service sets one functional cookie, `tiler_token`
(HttpOnly, valid for one hour), which authorizes your browser to load map tiles
from our tile server. It is strictly necessary for the Service and is not used
for tracking.

**No tracking.** The Service uses no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking
cookies. We do not sell or share personal data for marketing.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR)

- Providing the Service you request - account management, campaigns,
  annotations: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Security, abuse prevention, and operational logging: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, based
  on our legitimate interest in operating the Service securely.

## 4. Recipients and processors

**Google Firebase Authentication** (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) handles
sign-in with email/password or a Google account. Google processes your sign-in
data under its own terms; data may be transferred to the United States under
the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. See
Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

**Google Fonts.** Web fonts are loaded from Google's font CDN, so your IP
address reaches Google when a page loads. No account data is sent.

**Microsoft Azure** (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.) hosts the Service. All
Service data is stored in the Azure region West Europe (Netherlands), within
the EU.

**Imagery providers.** For some imagery, your browser loads map tiles directly
from the provider's servers (for example Microsoft Planetary Computer or
Planet). In those requests your IP address reaches the provider, as with any
website you visit. No account data is sent.

**Google Earth Engine** is used by our servers for some computations. These
requests are made server-side and contain no personal data.

**Other members.** Your email address and display name are visible to
administrators and, depending on settings, to other members of the
organizations and campaigns you belong to.

We do not pass personal data to anyone else unless required by law.
