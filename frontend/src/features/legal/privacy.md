# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31

This notice explains what personal data STACNotator (the "Service") collects,
why, and what you can do about it.

https://github.com/RAAPID-ORG/stacnotator/issues if the matter is not private.

## What we hold, and why

**Your account.** Your email address, the username you choose, an identifier
from the sign-in provider, the version of the Terms of Service you accepted,
and the date the account was created. We need these to give you an account and
to know what you are allowed to see.

**Your work.** The annotations, labels, form answers, comments, campaigns,
projects, imagery configurations, and workspace layouts you create, each
recorded against your account. For annotation tasks we also record how long you
were actively working on each one, which campaign administrators see as part of
that campaign's progress statistics.

**API keys you add.** If you connect an imagery provider with your own
credentials, we store that key encrypted and use it only to fetch imagery for
your campaigns.

**Technical records.** Our servers and our hosting provider record the usual
request metadata, including your IP address, the pages and endpoints requested,
and timing. We keep these for a short period to operate the Service, to
diagnose faults, and to detect abuse.

**In your browser.** One cookie, `tiler_token`, which is HttpOnly and lasts one
hour and authorizes your browser to load map tiles. Your sign-in session, held
by the Firebase authentication library. Your interface preferences, such as
panel layouts. All of these are needed for the Service to work. There is no
analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

The legal basis for holding your account, your work, and your keys is Art.
6(1)(b) GDPR: it is what providing the Service to you requires. The legal basis
for the technical records is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in
running the Service securely and reliably.

## Who else sees it

**Other people using the Service.** Your username is visible to other members
of the organizations, projects, and campaigns you belong to, and is shown next
to work you contribute there. Administrators of those organizations and
projects, and platform administrators, can also see your email address. A
campaign's administrators can export that campaign's annotations, and the
export names the annotator who made each one by email address.

**Google.** Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, so Google receives
your email address and holds your credentials
(https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Microsoft.** The Service is hosted on Microsoft Azure in the West Europe and
North Europe regions.

**Imagery providers.** For some imagery your browser requests map tiles
directly from the provider, so that provider sees your IP address and which
tiles you asked for.

We give personal data to no one else, we do not sell it, and we do not use it
for advertising.

## Where your data is

Your account and your work are stored in the European Union, in the Azure
regions named above. Some of the providers described above may process data
outside the European Economic Area, and those transfers rest on an adequacy
decision or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Annotations published as research data

Each campaign's administrators can turn on research sharing for their campaign,
and the Terms of Service describe what that means. Annotations published this
way carry no name, email address, username, or other identifier of who made
them, so what is published is not personal data. Once something has been
published under an open license it cannot be recalled.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and the work attached to it for as long as your account
exists. Technical records are kept short-term.

If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and the personal data held
with it; annotations that a campaign depends on may be kept without any link to
you. We keep a one-way fingerprint of your sign-in identifier, and nothing
else, so that visiting the Service again does not silently recreate the account
you asked us to remove.

Your sign-in account with the authentication provider is held separately from
your STACNotator account. Tell us if you want that deleted too and we will
remove it.

We do not promise to retain any account, project, or annotation for a
particular period, and the Terms of Service explain that the Service should not
be your only copy of anything important.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to
restrict or object to what we do with it, or to receive it in a portable form.
Write to [PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL] and we will answer within one month.

If you are in the EEA or the UK you may also complain to your data protection
supervisory authority.

## Children

You must be at least 13 years old to use the Service. It is not directed to
children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Changes

We update this notice when the way the Service handles data changes. The
current version and the date it took effect are shown at the top.
